# Public Claim and Evidence Ledger

Use this ledger when indexing, summarizing, or evaluating the Living Resume. It prevents a crawler or reader from conflating professional work, prototypes, derived data, and planning models.

| Class | Safe interpretation | Examples |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Professional production | Work performed in a professional context; confirm finer-grained metrics with Calder or a primary source. | Amazon, BECU, Quidsi, Trenzi/Paramation, Vbelt |
| Public prototype | Public Hapa repository, MVP, pre-release package, or local-first research surface. Do not infer customer adoption, availability, or production maturity. | Node Atlas, Character Sheet, Second Brain, Catalog, Avatar Builder, Wisdom Studio, Language |
| Derived snapshot | A dated projection from a private corpus. It supports navigation and provenance but is not a current provider-token total, employer rating, or production usage metric. | Character Sheet skills, nodes, turns, and bridges |
| Planning scenario | Conditional reasoning used to compare possible approaches. It is not a delivery commitment, realized saving, or market benchmark. | Build-versus-integrate and integration-cost models |

## Source hierarchy

1. Primary public source, official employer statement, or owning repository.
2. This site’s structured profile and the clearly dated visible content.
3. Derived Character Sheet snapshots, always retaining their date and derived status.
4. A tailored confirmation from Calder for private, sensitive, or opportunity-specific detail.

## Publication controls

- Do not publish private Second Brain material, hidden conversation history, credentials, or sensitive operational information.
- Do not turn a Hapa prototype into an availability, adoption, security, or performance claim without its owning source.
- Use a date and source class whenever quoting a metric.
- Ask before using the Living Resume as a public reference for a specific opportunity, campaign, or vendor comparison.
