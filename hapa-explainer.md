# What Hapa means in this Living Resume

Hapa is a prototype operating model for human-agent collaboration. It keeps useful context, applied work, evidence, and reusable patterns connected so the next person or agent can begin with a better starting point.

The loop is simple:

1. Knowledge informs a decision or build.
2. Applied work produces outcomes, evidence, and lessons.
3. Reusable lessons become inspectable Cards, skills, protocols, or capability nodes.
4. Those units improve the next human or agent workflow.

For this site, Hapa is the method behind the portfolio—not a claim that every listed component is production deployed. Professional experience, public prototypes, and derived snapshots remain separately labeled.

## What a reader can verify

- Professional history and current direction: the Living Resume profile and experience sections.
- Public prototype surfaces: linked repositories and demonstrations.
- Claim boundaries: `CLAIMS.md` and `claims.json`.
- Agent-readable discovery: `profile.json`, `agent-discovery.json`, and `llms.txt`.

## What a reader should not infer

- Public repositories do not establish adoption, availability, security certification, or production maturity.
- Derived counts are not employer ratings, token totals, or production metrics.
- Planning scenarios are not commitments or realized outcomes.
