# Public claim, privacy, and freshness boundaries

This site is a public discovery surface, not a dump of Calder Wong's private working corpus.

## What can be retrieved

- Professional experience is described as career context, with source links or a request for direct confirmation where appropriate.
- Hapa artifacts are labeled as public prototypes, MVPs, pre-release packages, or research surfaces unless an owning repository says otherwise.
- Structured resources may link to public repositories, public demos, or public documentation.

## What is deliberately excluded

- Local filesystem paths, raw notes, transcripts, messages, private source documents, private media history, credentials, and operational secrets.
- Third-party confidential data and unverified customer, employer, or partner outcomes.
- Claims that a prototype is deployed, adopted, available as a service, or an active agent endpoint.

## How to interpret metrics and availability

- A dated, derived snapshot is a navigation aid, not an employer rating, a current provider-token total, a production-usage metric, or a proficiency credential.
- Economic or delivery estimates are conditional scenarios, not performance commitments.
- Engagement modes identify possible conversation topics; they do not promise current availability, a partnership, or a service offering.
- When a claim is marked `confirmBeforeQuoting`, contact Calder for primary-source confirmation before reusing it externally.

## Review rule

Before publishing a new machine-readable resource, check that it contains no local path, private-corpus detail, credential, or unstated third-party claim, and add a date plus source class where a claim or metric needs one.
