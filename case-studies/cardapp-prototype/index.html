CardAppPrototype: from product brief to a one-day demonstrable build

This case study documents an AI-assisted React and Vite card-management prototype: why it was built, what it demonstrated, and which patterns are reusable in future product work.

What it demonstrates

A concrete product brief can become an inspectable prototype when product framing, interface decisions, and implementation feedback remain connected. The value is in the demonstrable learning loop—not a claim of production deployment.

Reusable lessons

Keep product intent visible in the build; surface important states and exceptions; and preserve a small, inspectable path from requirements to interface behavior. These are patterns to validate and adapt, not universal prescriptions.

Evidence boundary

CardAppPrototype is a public prototype. It should not be presented as employer work, a production financial service, or evidence of adoption, revenue, or operational performance.
